PADV project · Agriculture 49

Predicting crop disease from field conditions

An early-warning dashboard built on your Decision Tree and SVM models. Explore the data, compare the models, get a crop recommendation, and scan a leaf photo for disease risk.

Pipeline

As implemented in your notebook

  1. LoadKaggle agriculture CSVs from archive.zip
  2. CleanMedian / mode imputation, label encoding
  3. SplitStratified 80:20, random_state 42
  4. TrainDecision Tree (depth 5) and RBF SVM, scaled
  5. EvaluateAccuracy, precision, recall, F1, confusion matrix
  6. ServeBoth models run live on the next pages

Your two exported models recommend a crop (12 classes) from soil, weather, nutrients and season. They do not read images. The leaf page therefore pairs a colour analysis of the photo with a disease-risk model trained on the repo's disease_dataset.csv, using the same Decision Tree and SVM approach.
